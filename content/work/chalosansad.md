After the 2026 NEET exam leak affected 2.27 million students, a protest movement grew around it — a 45-day sit-in at Delhi's Jantar Mantar, a march toward Parliament, and months of negotiation with the government. Chalo Sansad is a site I've been maintaining to track it day by day, from the exam leak itself through to where things stand now.

It leans on primary sources rather than a single narrative: most claims link back to original reporting, and contested events — police response, force used, internet shutdowns — get a "same events, two accounts" treatment pairing the official version against protester and independent accounts. A separate tracker checks government promises against what's actually been done. The premise is simple: an informed crowd is harder to ignore than a loud one.
