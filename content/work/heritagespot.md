HeritageSpot is a guide to India's spiritual and cultural landmarks — over 200 curated sites across every region, covering their history, architecture, religious significance, and the deities, saints, and festivals associated with them. It's built for pilgrims, tourists, history enthusiasts, and anyone curious about the country's cultural fabric.

Everything works offline, so it holds up on the road and away from signal. It's available in ten languages, and covers the practical side too — opening hours, dress codes, accessibility, nearby sites worth a detour, and directions to get there.
